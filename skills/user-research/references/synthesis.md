# Traceable synthesis

Toolkit workflow:

1. Inventory the supplied sessions, artifacts, dates, participant contexts and research questions. Disclose unavailable or incomplete sources.
2. Extract observations with session/source locations. Keep quotations exact, brief and non-identifying. Distinguish observed actions, participant statements and analyst interpretations.
3. Group related evidence without erasing exceptions. Check whether several notes describe the same incident before counting them as independent observations.
4. State each finding with supporting cases, contradictory cases and its scope. Report counts with a denominator only when the source supports both; small convenience samples do not establish population prevalence.
5. Separate a finding from a recommendation. Name the uncertain assumption connecting them and what further observation could challenge it.

Example: two participants selected Archive while trying to remove a draft. This supports investigating the label in those tasks. It does not establish that most users misunderstand archiving or that renaming it improves retention.

Do not report significance, causal effects, saturation or representativeness without the evidence needed for that claim. Participant opinions, usage analytics and task observations can disagree; retain that disagreement.

Research lifecycle reference: [GOV.UK user research manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research). These synthesis instructions are toolkit conventions rather than a claim to reproduce the manual's complete method.
