# Design validation

## Match the question to the evidence

Toolkit distinctions:

- Inspection establishes a visible property or behaviour in the inspected conditions. It can identify a plausible usability risk, not its prevalence.
- Usability observation shows how the participants handled the supplied tasks and conditions. It does not by itself establish population-wide frequency or a causal conversion improvement.
- Analytics can reveal abandonment or error patterns but may not explain intent or cause.
- A controlled experiment can address a defined comparison if assignment, measurement and sample are adequate. Do not invent sample-size or significance claims.
- Automated accessibility checks cover a subset of issues. Manual input and assistive-technology checks answer different questions.

## Propose a small, useful study

Define the uncertain decision and the target audience. Choose a realistic task with a clear completion condition, without naming the control being tested. Record completion, wrong turns, consequential errors, recovery and participant explanations. Avoid leading prompts and separate observed behaviour from the researcher's interpretation.

Example: ask someone to correct a submitted delivery address, rather than instructing them to use the edit icon. Establish whether they can find the route, understand its limits and recover if editing is unavailable. State which result would support changing the design.

Use existing consent, privacy and research processes. Propose recruitment or outreach without conducting it unless authorised. Choose participants and rounds to address the uncertainty; a fixed participant count is not a universal proof of validation.

Sources: [GOV.UK moderated usability testing](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/user-research/using-moderated-usability-testing) for planning and running task-based sessions, and [NN/G heuristic evaluation](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-to-conduct-a-heuristic-evaluation/) for inspection as a complementary method. The distinctions and example above are toolkit synthesis.
